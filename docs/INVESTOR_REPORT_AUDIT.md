- **Partial:** Useful evidence exists, but coverage, provenance, or confidence
  needs improvement.
- **Blocked:** The field cannot be completed honestly without a new data source
  or user-provided evidence.

## Prioritized investor work queue

1. **Expose and reconcile the score model.** Make the 95-point components and
   100-point display agree, disclose deductions, and represent evidence
   completeness independently.
2. **Return individual rental comps.** Add sanitized rows with source, date,
   distance, property facts, similarity, and asking-versus-leased provenance.
3. **Make assumptions auditable and saveable.** Align financing slider values,
   add rate provenance, support real expense overrides, and preserve named
   scenarios beside the original analysis.
4. **Model full invested cash.** Add itemized closing costs, capex, special
   assessments, cumulative cash shortfalls, renewals, disposition costs, and
   tax-sensitive return views.
5. **License Ontario market evidence.** Use the planned TRREB/Ontario coverage
   for local sold comps and appreciation; never adapt Tacoma sample rows into
   local-looking evidence.
6. **Improve risk and document evidence.** Cache extraction by description
   hash/version and ingest status certificates, inspections, reserve studies,
   title, permits, and verified municipal sources.
7. **Correct STR provenance.** Treat the current legality copy as a dated static
   snapshot and withhold revenue comparisons until real STR data and complete
   cost inputs are connected.
8. **Persist due diligence and exports.** Save checklist evidence and validate
   account/PDF/share behavior after owner-approved deployment.

  longer writes them.

  than hidden behind a single score.

- Unknown year and parking remain unknown rather than becoming plausible facts.

  support Vaughan appreciation.

  explained rather than filled with fabricated figures.

## Status key

- **Working:** The field is backed by the stated source and renders honestly.
- **Partial:** Useful evidence exists, but coverage, provenance, or confidence
  needs improvement.
- **Blocked:** The field cannot be completed honestly without a new data source
  or user-provided evidence.

## Prioritized personal-use work queue

1. **License and integrate Ontario sold comparables.** This unlocks an honest
   FMV band and aggregate home score. It must use real Ontario coverage and must
   never adapt Tacoma sample rows into local-looking evidence.
2. **Correct score completeness.** Replace the 10/10 silent-risk state with a
   neutral evidence state and expose every weighted component.
3. **Make cost assumptions editable.** Add working financing, CMHC, insurance,
   utilities, maintenance, and cash-to-close inputs with visible provenance.
4. **Improve field-level provenance.** Distinguish a confirmed zero from a
   missing value for parking and other normalized listing facts.
5. **Add routes and school boundaries.** Replace inferred travel minutes and
   nearest-school assumptions with routed distance and verified assignments.
6. **Cache risk extraction and add document evidence.** Key extraction by
   description hash/version, then accept inspection and status-certificate
   evidence without allowing listing silence to become clearance.
7. **Persist buyer due diligence.** Save checklist answers, notes, dates, and
   supporting documents.

## Already corrected during this audit

- The overall score is withheld when verified local pricing evidence is absent.
- Live reports never synthesize a fair-market-value band from the asking price.
- Tacoma sales remain labelled as provider sample coverage and do not feed the
  Vaughan valuation.
- Verdict prose is deterministic backend output; Sonnet no longer writes it.
- Twelve real listing photos render without invented frames or counts.
- Unknown build year and parking remain unknown rather than becoming plausible
  facts.
- Listed property tax and backend tax estimates retain their provenance.
- School distance is labelled straight-line and attendance boundaries are not
  claimed.
- Unsupported market trends remain em dashes with “No source connected.”
- SunScout states its facade assumption and omitted unknown-height buildings.
- Empty risk extraction is presented as a limited wording scan.
- Inactive agent-email and referral promises were removed or labelled
  unavailable.
